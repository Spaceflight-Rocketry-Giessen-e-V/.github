# Spaceflight Rocketry Gießen e.V.

Student rocketry from Gießen, Germany. Founded 2023, over 60 members, most of them from
the Technische Hochschule Mittelhessen (THM).

We build experimental rockets to put lecture hall theory into practice. Almost everything
is made in house: carbon and glass fibre airframes wound on a machine we built ourselves,
flight computers we design, populate and solder ourselves, and parachutes we sew
ourselves. Our telemetry system is open hardware.

## Our rockets

| | Status | Size | Altitude |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PIPE** | flown, August 2024 | 105 mm, 1.5 m | 880 m reached, 500 m planned |
| **PIPE 2** | flown, April 2026 | 105 mm, 1.7 m | 358 m, partial success |
| **ARCHER Mk. 1** | in build | 204 mm, 3.6 m, ~36 kg | 3000 m, single stage |
| **ARCHER Mk. 2** | in design | 204 mm, 6 m | 9000 m, two stages |

PIPE 2 was a learning flight. Part of the multi-stage recovery system failed, the rocket
landed hard and survived with minimal damage, and telemetry held the link the whole way
down without a single dropout.

## Open source

**[Telemetry](https://github.com/Spaceflight-Rocketry-Giessen-e-V/Telemetry)** is our
bidirectional rocket telemetry system, released as open hardware under CERN-OHL-S v2:
KiCad schematics and PCBs, firmware, ground station software, antenna designs and
documentation.

Dual band 169 / 869 MHz, Radiocrafts RC232 modules, range up to 20 km, and a modular
ground station taking up to four daughterboards.

## How we are organised

A **Vorstand**, a **Technisches Gremium** led by the Oberster Technischer Leiter, and five
**Arbeitsgruppen**: Antrieb & Startsysteme, Raketenkörper, Elektronik, Landesysteme,
Nutzlast.
